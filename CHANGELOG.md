## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-11

### Added

- Reproducible TGAZ/CHGIS data POC and provenance-aware normalization.
- Viewport-driven MapLibre interface with an exact-year timeline.
- Manual historical layer controls, co-location grouping, defensive details,
  display modes, and modern-reference fallback.
- Source coverage, identity-safety, coordinate, and source-upgrade QA tools.
